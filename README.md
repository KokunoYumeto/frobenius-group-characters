# G. Frobenius, papers on group characters (1896–1903) — German text and English translation

Ten papers by Ferdinand Georg Frobenius from the *Sitzungsberichte der Königlich Preußischen Akademie der
Wissenschaften zu Berlin*, in which he created the theory of group characters and group representations: the German
text and an English translation.

## Completeness

Each of the ten papers is complete. This is a selection: Frobenius's later papers on characters and representations,
including those written with Issai Schur, are not included.

The German follows the printed spelling and notation. Running heads, page numbers and the page breaks of the print
are not reproduced, so page references in the text refer to the *Sitzungsberichte*.

## How it was made

The German text was transcribed from the scans and translated into English by AI (OpenAI's ChatGPT), page by page,
with each page compared visually with the scan.

## Checks

- During production a few readings where the scan is hard to read were flagged for checking: in *Theorie der
  hyperkomplexen Größen*, formulas (8.) and (10.) of §9, the words "der Ordnung r" in §10 and formulas (4.)–(5.) of
  §15; in part II, formula (2.) of §2, the readings z = zx⁰ + … and |S(z)| = zⁿ in §4, and the construction of
  f<sub>λ</sub> before (11\*.) in §5.
- On 27 October 2026 Claude Opus 5.5 (Anthropic) compared printed page 25 of *Über die Primfactoren der
  Gruppendeterminante* and page 9 of *Über die Charaktere der symmetrischen Gruppe* with the scans, and the English of
  the latter page with the German. The formulas agree with the print. The transcription had "characteristisch" where
  the print has "charakteristisch", in all five places where the word occurs (checked on the scan at pages 17 and
  25); this is corrected. Some words that the print sets in italics are roman in the edition.
- The other pages have not had a second check.
